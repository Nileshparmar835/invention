# Colorful paper-cut animation direction

The episode is staged like a tactile storybook collage. Use a warm cream canvas and flat colorful paper shapes with offset shadows. Outline faces, objects and buildings in dark teal, with coral, apricot, mustard and seafoam accents. Daylight and high contrast keep actions legible on a phone.

Characters have readable colored faces and separate head, arm and accessory motion. The cut-paper edges, small printed flecks and stepped shadows make physical objects feel handmade. Short three-word sticker captions sit close to the bottom with an active word highlighted. The original drama comes from the action and camera staging.

Build a new setting for each new script. Keep the palette, character proportions, tactile surface and sticker caption treatment consistent across episodes. Synchronize each important prop with the onset of its spoken word. Draw anticipation, contact and consequence. Use shot changes for meaningful story beats.

The nine supplied clips informed narrative timing and visual cause and effect. Their dark palette, shadowed round faces and upper single-word captions are replaced by this project's colorful presentation.
